What each particle does

Batchim rule: 을/이/은 follow a noun ending in a consonant (받침); 를/가/는 follow a noun ending in a vowel. 에 and 에서 never change.
누구 / 누가: 누구 + 가 contracts to 누가, so the subject form of “who” is its own word — those two are offered only on the 누구/누가 cards.
